ASMI-Cameroun in Addis Ababa for Africa CDC youth events and CPHIA 2026

Youth Pre-Conference: 21–22 November · CPHIA: 23–27 November 2026

Addis Ababa, Ethiopia · 22 November 2026

Learning and connecting across Africa

The President and Executive Director of ASMI-Cameroun, Fabrice Achu Ngando, is in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, for the Africa CDC Youth Pre-Conference (21–22 November 2026), ahead of the International Conference on Public Health in Africa (CPHIA 2026, 23–27 November).

His participation offers an opportunity to listen, learn, and exchange perspectives with youth leaders and public-health professionals from across the continent. For ASMI-Cameroun, these conversations are relevant to the links between community wellbeing, safe migration, prevention of human trafficking, protection of vulnerable people and responsible use of evidence.

Connecting the experience to ASMI's work

ASMI-Cameroun focuses on data-driven prevention, survivor-centred protection and evidence-based advocacy. Learning from African public-health discussions can help inform our ongoing work on awareness, risk mapping and safe referral practices, starting in Moungo Division, Cameroon.

We plan to share a factual update after the conference on lessons relevant to our programmes. Participation in these events does not imply a formal partnership, institutional endorsement, or funding relationship with Africa CDC, the African Union, or any other event organisation.

Data confidentiality

We work with sensitive information about people at risk. We collect only what we need, keep it restricted to authorised people, and publish totals and patterns, never names. Surveys are anonymous, and no survivor data appears on this website.
