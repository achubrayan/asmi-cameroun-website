Digital Safe Route

Digital Safe Route: taking prevention into the community.

A pilot in Moungo that teaches people how to check a job offer, collects anonymous data on the scams they have met, and uses what we learn to improve the next sessions.

Pilot project · MoungoDigital Safe Route
  • Teaching people to check a job offer
  • Anonymous data on the scams they meet
  • Pilot budget: 700,000 FCFA
Pledge your supportView Campaign ProgressSupport via WhatsApp

Pilot logic

Teach first. Learn from it.

We want to learn what works in Moungo before taking it any further.

01

Community Awareness

Open sessions on recruitment risks, fake jobs and safer migration choices.

02

Verification Skills

Hands-on ways to check an employer, a recruiter, a contract or a website.

03

Field Evidence

Anonymous data on the scams people meet, so each round of outreach is better aimed.

Data confidentiality

We work with sensitive information about people at risk. We collect only what we need, keep it restricted to authorised people, and publish totals and patterns, never names. Surveys are anonymous, and no survivor data appears on this website.
