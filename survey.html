Before you start

What you should know.

  • It is anonymous. We do not ask who you are.
  • Taking part is voluntary.
  • Please do not type your name, phone number, ID or passport number, or an exact address in any text box.
  • We look for patterns, not people. We publish totals and trends, never individual answers.
  • Your answers are handled under our Safeguarding and Data Protection Policy.

This is not a help line

If you or someone you know is in danger now, contact the local authorities or a protection service you trust. For other questions, write to us at contact@asmi-cameroun.org.

What happens next

From your answer to action.

Collect

Answers arrive in KoboToolbox, the tool we use for field data.

Clean

We remove errors and duplicates, and check the quality of the data.

Analyse

We look for patterns: scam types, routes, places and trends.

Act

We use the findings for awareness, outreach planning and advocacy.

Data confidentiality

We work with sensitive information about people at risk. We collect only what we need, keep it restricted to authorised people, and publish totals and patterns, never names. Surveys are anonymous, and no survivor data appears on this website.
