16 October 2026 • Programme

Our programme: Safe Choices, Safe Return

Our Digital Safe Route community pilot has grown into a 12-month programme that trains community ambassadors, supports returned survivors of trafficking, and lets survivors who choose it become ambassadors. It is a proposal seeking 11,520,000 FCFA (US$20,000); no donor has committed yet. Community fundraising to launch it in Littoral, starting in Moungo, remains open, with a target of 2,880,000 FCFA (about US$5,000).

3 October 2026 • Governance

Safeguarding and Data Protection Policy approved

The Executive Bureau approved Version 1.0 under Decision DEC-017/ASMI-CM/BE/2026. It covers safeguarding, child protection, survivor confidentiality, consent, data security and how concerns are reported. Mancho Tebo Salif is designated as Data Protection Officer.

20 September 2026 • Governance

General Assembly strengthens accountability and preparation for community outreach

At the General Assembly, members went over responsibilities, monthly contributions, learning and participation, and prepared our first community outreach. Attendance and action points were recorded.

Data confidentiality

We work with sensitive information about people at risk. We collect only what we need, keep it restricted to authorised people, and publish totals and patterns, never names. Surveys are anonymous, and no survivor data appears on this website.
