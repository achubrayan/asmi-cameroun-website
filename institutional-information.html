Legal & Institutional Information

Official legal identity, governance, registration, institutional contacts and data-protection commitments of African Safe Migration Initiative – Cameroun (ASMI-Cameroun).

African Safe Migration Initiative – Cameroun (ASMI-Cameroun) is a declared Cameroonian nonprofit civil society association working to prevent human trafficking, cyber-enabled employment scams, fraudulent international job offers and unsafe migration, while supporting survivor-centred protection, dignified reintegration and evidence-based advocacy.

ASMI-Cameroun combines data-driven prevention, survivor-informed practice and institutional advocacy to strengthen safe migration and protection in Cameroon and, progressively, across Africa.

Mandate

Our Legal Purpose

ASMI-Cameroun’s principal statutory purpose is to prevent and combat transnational and intra-African human trafficking, including trafficking linked to cyber-scam networks, fraudulent employment offers and related forms of exploitation, while supporting the dignified reintegration of survivors.

Our work is humanitarian, social and focused on the protection of human rights.

Statutes

Statutory Objectives

Intelligence and Prevention

To map, analyse and document trafficking risks, methods and emerging patterns, including the operating methods of trafficking and cyber-scam networks affecting African communities.

Institutional Advocacy and Coordination

To contribute evidence, information and responsible referrals that support coordination with competent institutions involved in victim protection, safe migration and related responses.

Survivor-Centred Reintegration

To provide or facilitate non-financial support networks, peer mentorship and appropriate legal, psychosocial and reintegration-oriented guidance for survivors.

Safe Migration Awareness

To develop and disseminate awareness and verification tools relating to international employment opportunities, recruitment risks, fraudulent job offers and safe migration.

Data and Technological Innovation

To develop and use data-analysis approaches for risk mapping, trend identification, prevention intelligence and early-warning systems relating to trafficking and cyber-enabled exploitation.

Method

Our Strategic Approach

01

DATA-DRIVEN PREVENTION

We use responsible data collection, risk analysis, community intelligence, scam typologies and public education to identify emerging threats and strengthen prevention.

02

SURVIVOR-CENTRED PROTECTION & REINTEGRATION

We promote safe referral, dignity, informed consent, confidentiality, peer support and appropriate pathways for survivor protection and reintegration.

03

EVIDENCE-BASED ADVOCACY

We translate responsible data and field evidence into practical engagement with public authorities, civil society organisations, international organisations, diplomatic missions and other relevant stakeholders.

Through this model, ASMI-Cameroun is developing its role as a Data Sentinel organisation for safe migration and anti-trafficking prevention.

Governance

Governance

ASMI-Cameroun is governed through its General Assembly and Executive Bureau in accordance with its Statutes and approved internal governance instruments.

The General Assembly is the supreme governing body of the association.

The Executive Bureau is responsible for the administration and management of the association within the authority granted by the Statutes and decisions of the competent governing bodies.

The statutory Executive Bureau structure comprises:

  • President / Executive Director
  • Secretary General
  • Treasurer
  • Programme Officer
  • Community Mobilisation Officer

The mandate of the Executive Bureau is three years and is renewable in accordance with the Statutes.

Confidentiality

Data Protection, Privacy & Confidentiality

ASMI-Cameroun recognises that responsible handling of personal and survivor-related information is fundamental to its mission.

  • Collect only information that serves a legitimate and defined protection, prevention, programme or institutional purpose.
  • Apply appropriate confidentiality and access controls to personal and sensitive information.
  • Protect survivor identities and avoid unnecessary disclosure of identifiable case information.
  • Obtain informed and appropriate consent where personal information, stories, photographs or other identifying material may be collected or used.
  • Prioritise anonymised and aggregated information for research, dashboards, advocacy and public reporting wherever possible.
  • Limit access to sensitive information to authorised persons with a legitimate need to know.
  • Do not disclose personal data without appropriate consent except where disclosure is lawfully required.
  • Apply safeguards designed to reduce risks of unauthorised access, misuse, re-identification or disclosure.

Survivor safety, dignity and informed choice take priority over publicity, fundraising, storytelling and data collection.

Safeguarding

Safeguarding

ASMI-Cameroun is committed to survivor-centred, trauma-informed and non-stigmatising approaches.

We do not publicly disclose survivor names, identity documents, addresses, medical information, immigration information, exact locations, photographs or other identifiable case information without an appropriate lawful basis and informed consent.

ASMI-Cameroun does not guarantee visas, employment, repatriation, financial assistance, immigration outcomes, medical assistance or other forms of support unless the organisation has confirmed the necessary resources, mandate and authority.

Where ASMI-Cameroun cannot directly provide a service, the organisation seeks, where appropriate and possible, to facilitate responsible information or referral to competent actors.

Accountability

Institutional Transparency

ASMI-Cameroun is committed to responsible governance, safeguarding, financial accountability, accurate public communication and evidence-based programme management.

References to partnerships, funding relationships, beneficiaries, programme results and institutional recognition are made only where the relevant status or evidence has been confirmed.

Contact with an organisation does not, by itself, constitute a formal partnership, funding relationship or institutional endorsement.

ASMI-Cameroun seeks to maintain accurate, current and verifiable institutional information across its official documents and digital platforms.

Important Legal Clarification

ASMI-Cameroun is an independent nonprofit civil society association.

Its declaration and registration under Cameroonian law should not be interpreted as government accreditation, government endorsement or affiliation with the Government of Cameroon.

References to ministries, embassies, international organisations, civil society organisations or other institutions on this website do not imply a formal partnership unless such a relationship is expressly identified as formal and supported by the relevant agreement or institutional decision.
